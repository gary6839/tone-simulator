Guitar Tone Filter Simulator

  • Move the Tone and Volume knobs and watch the graph. The curve shows how strongly each frequency reaches the amp. Where it drops on the right, the treble is being cut.
  • Press Play to hear a slightly overdriven guitar through the current settings. Keep it playing while you move the knobs: rolling Tone down smooths the fizz, and rolling Volume down cleans up the distortion, just like a real guitar into a driven amp.
  • Cutoff is the frequency where the treble has dropped 3 dB. A lower number means a darker sound.
  • Setup & Circuit starts open. Use it to match a guitar (pickup, pot value, tone cap, wiring style and treble bleed) and see how it's wired.
  • Treble bleed: pick a style (Cap only, Parallel/Duncan or Series/Kinman), then a recipe. A ★ marks recipes suited to your pot value. Roll the Volume down to hear what it keeps.
  • Close Setup (tap its header or the Done button) so the graph and knobs fit on screen together. Tap the header again any time to reopen it.

This is a simplified model. Real guitars vary with cable length, amp and component tolerances, but the trends match what players hear.
